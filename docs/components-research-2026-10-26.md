# Components research, 26 October 2026

This note covers the bounded research behind the October 2026 buyer-UX and components update: carbon wheelsets, groupset prices and one wide-tire aero check. Facts live in the linked data records; this note records selection choices and blocked routes so later batches do not repeat them.

## Carbon wheelsets

Added from exact official specification pages, each with a dated official-store price in its original currency:

- Magene EXAR Ultra DB508: independent review by the5krunner (unsponsored, 83%).
- Farsports EVO C6 (2026): Cyclingnews wind-tunnel test, 30 July 2026. The article does not state the model year.
- YOELEO SAT C35 DB PRO NxT SL2: Cyclists Hub named the NxT SL2 range best value after testing the C60, not the C35. The site uses affiliate links.
- 9Velo CD58 Wide (2025) and ICAN G25 SL 700C: added for complete official specifications and fit data. No independent test was read, and maker-hosted store ratings are not used as evidence.

Photos: eight wheelsets now have reviewed exact-model photos from the makers' own galleries. The three Elitewheels rows have none because elite-wheels.com timed out from both the command line and the approved browser. An archived copy also failed.

Left out:

- Winspace Lún GRAPID 700: its successor, GRAPID 2, was published on 29 September 2026, US stock was sold out, and the page gives no pressure or load limit.
- Light Bicycle Turbo 50 and AG48: prices come only from a configurator.
- CRW 5060: no official product page was found.
- Magene GR45 Pro, ICAN Aero 50 II and YOELEO C60/CS60: the C60 is the same platform as the C35 already added, and the road.cc CS60 coverage was sponsored. For the others, the evidence was weaker than for the chosen rows.

Hambini review pages sit behind a Cloudflare challenge and were not opened.

## Groupset prices

WheelTop's official store prices (in euros) were re-observed for the EDS TX and GeX packages. Mainland China listings were not recorded:

- Taobao search asked for a login.
- SMZDM returned an anti-bot check.
- JD item prices are login-walled.

These routes are human-only. Unchanged mainland observations keep their original dates.

## Wide-tire aero

The SEKA Spear stays out of the wide-clearance aero category. SEKA's current pages and catalog give 32 mm for both frames (see `docs/wide-clearance-ledger-2026-10-25.md`), and this batch required more than 35 mm. No bike data changed. That ledger also lists the aero options above 34 mm and their 2× limits.

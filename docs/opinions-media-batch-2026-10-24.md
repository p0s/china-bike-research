## Sources and routes

- Public Reddit threads and comments, linked by canonical post or comment permalink. Opinions are paraphrased; usernames are not recorded.
- YouTube reviews, read through the video's own transcript panel and description. Supplied products, loans, affiliate codes and paid-promotion flags are recorded as each video's relationship and disclosure.
- Weight Weenies returned a security-verification page; that route was stopped, not bypassed.
- Bilibili search was tried for Chinese-market owners but returned no exact-model results usable without transcripts.

## Exactness exclusions

Opinions were dropped when the post did not identify the exact model or generation: Carbonda CFR707 and CFR505 posts and rebadged CFR696 frames sold under other brands; rim-brake Falath Pro, Falath Evo and Falath EXP reviews; pre-Altera Yoeleo G21 posts; Twitter Gravel V3 posts that do not identify the 2025 T47 generation; the separately named Winspace T1600 Ultra; and seller or brand posts. Thin praise without substance and not-yet-ridden orders were also left out.

## Images

No new product photos were added. Wikimedia Commons and Flickr Creative Commons searches found no exact-model photos for these ten models, and no brand press kit with documented reuse terms was found. Under the current media rule attribution alone is not permission, so the existing reviewed photos remain and the gap is open. AVIF encoding was not needed because no new derivative was produced.

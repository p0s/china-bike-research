# Image gap inventory — 2026-08-17

This is the round-two image audit of the full catalog and candidate set. It is a provenance inventory, not a publication decision.

## Snapshot

- 35 published platforms; all 35 have one primary image record.
- 61 total primary image records: 35 published-platform targets and 26 candidate targets.
- 59 remote records and 2 project-owned local fallbacks.
- Subject accuracy: 37 exact-variant, 15 exact-platform, 3 same-model/different-market-build, 4 same-platform, and 2 illustrative.
- Eleven candidate targets gained exact official product-page images: Quick Pro ER:ONE, UR:ONE, XR:ONE and GR:ONE GRX Di2; SAVA A7L Pro 2026; Specialized Tarmac SL8 Comp; Trek Madone SL 5 Gen 8; X-LAB GT8; and Winspace SLC3, T1550 Gen 2 and Agile.

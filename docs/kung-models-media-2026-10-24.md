## Photos

36 photos (72 WebP files): 10 retailer photos and 8 XHS photos on the MACH frameset page, 2 builder photos on the MACH complete profile, 7 brand photos on MACH X and 9 photos on HORIZON. Each adds a distinct view: geometry diagram, cockpit chart, head tube, fork and caliper, seat cluster, rear triangle, frameset box contents, a deformed seatpost wedge, hoses through the headset, a price card with the HORIZON entry BOM, and paint options. Captions mark dealer, owner and brand builds as not catalog packages and state that tire photos are not clearance measurements.

The XHS images carry only the platform logo; none showed faces, plates, account identifiers or location labels. Images with riders' faces, shop signage or vehicles were left out.

Encoding: WebP through `scripts/optimize-image.mjs` (card ≤40 KB, detail ≤88 KB). A spot comparison at equal SSIM found AVIF (SVT-AV1) only 6–8% smaller than WebP for these photos, so the existing WebP pipeline was kept.

# Bike image gap inventory — 2026-11-03

On 2026-11-03, 67 catalog entries showed no verified photo: 65 research profiles and the two published LightCarbon SpeedZ records. This ledger names what was checked for each gap and why a photo was or was not added. It covers only the routes listed; "queued" means a later bounded batch still has to check that entry. A photo enters the site only as a reviewed local AVIF derivative under the media contract in `SPEC.md`. That requires an exact model, an HTTPS source page and media URL, credit, a rights note, a removal route and a privacy review. Originals stay in the local evidence archive, never in Git.

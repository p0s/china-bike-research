# Race and aero road bikes above 34 mm — 25–26 October 2026

Which race or aero road frames in the catalog take tyres wider than 34 mm, and does that hold with a 2× drivetrain? Values are manufacturer maxima unless stated otherwise. A tyre someone has fitted is not a maximum, and measured width grows with rim width.

## SEKA Spear: 32 mm for both frames

- SEKA's current Spear page lists **Max tire width (actual) 32 mm**, and the China page lists **32C front and rear** (`seka-spear-official-page-2026-10-25`, `seka-spear-cn-official-page-2026-10-25`). One specification block covers the RDC and Standard frames.
- The 2024–25 catalog's Spear section, which shows both frames, says "Support up to 32c" (`seka-spear-catalog-recheck-2026-10-25`). SEKA's development manual lists 28C tyres for its wind-tunnel runs.
- No official, dealer, review or owner source supports 35 mm. Klein Concept rode nominal 32 mm GP5000s. The 13 exact Spear posts captured on XHS show owner builds on 28 mm tyres and no clearance claim. A forum thread surfaced in search reports the same 32 mm ceiling; its posts were not opened, so it is not cited.
- **Conclusion:** the Spear is a 32 mm frame. A 35 mm tyre is outside SEKA's limit for the RDC and the Standard frame. SEKA's newer ExAero road frame is also recorded at 32 mm.

Painted frames weigh more than the 685 g headline, which is a size-M RDC frame without paint or metal parts. An XHS reviewer weighed a painted size-M dark-silver RDC frame at 835 g with metal parts, fork 406 g and Rapier bar 338 g; a paint shop's size-S RDC frame, stripped and clear-coated, weighed 718 g with a 345 g fork (`public-xhs-seka-spear-long-review-2026-10-26`, `public-xhs-seka-spear-rdc-refinished-weight-2026-10-26`).

**With 2×**, only the LCR018-D has documented room above 34 mm, and it is an endurance frame. The LCR020-D is the one race-geometry aero frame listed above 34 mm, but its 35C limit has no 2× confirmation. The Arden, Speedster and SSR need 1× to exceed 34 mm.

Outside race and aero road, existing records above 34 mm include the Argon 18 Krypton Pro all-road frame (recorded at 35C nominal / 38 mm actual with Shimano road Di2), the SCOTT Addict 40 (38 mm for the Addict road family, 105 Di2 build) and the Cervélo Áspero-5 aero gravel frame (45 mm). They were not re-checked in this batch.

## Discovery

A bounded set of about a dozen English and Chinese searches found no new race or aero road model above 34 mm. Results topped out at 32 mm for Winspace SLC3, T1550 and T1600, Elves Falath EVO, Tavelo Arow SL and Race (retailer listing), and Quick Pro ER:One and Evolve CIMA (review site). Global aero race frames cluster at 32–34 mm. No record was added; the two discovery slots stayed empty rather than padded with endurance or gravel bikes.

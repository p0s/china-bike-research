# Reviewed XHS model photos — 24 October 2026

This note records which reviewed XHS photos were added to model pages on 24 October 2026 and why many others were left out. It covers only the XHS posts already saved locally for earlier research; no new posts were opened. The original files stay outside this public repository. Each image record links its public source post and carries the original-file SHA-256, derivative hashes, sizes, alt text, credit, rights status and privacy review.

## What was reviewed

- 104 public posts in validated saved post files, containing 572 distinct image files. Posts about unrelated topics (travel, clothing, books, other products) were set aside from their titles and text before any image review.
- 89 posts with bicycle content were reviewed photo by photo. After removing exact copies and near-identical versions, and images already shown on the site, 388 candidate photos remained.
- 144 older images saved without a canonical post link were also checked. Nine were already published and eight duplicated photos above; the rest stay unpublished because their public source could not be established.

## Why the total is not higher

The goal was up to 1,000 new photos, but the saved posts contain far fewer useful ones. Of the 388 candidates, photos were left out when they showed faces, vehicle plates, recognizable landmarks, camera GPS stamps or creator handles that could not be removed with a small crop; carried large title overlays or were phone screenshots; showed a model that is not in the catalog (for example VOOK V9 or CAMP GX500) or whose generation could not be matched; repeated a view already published; or were text-only pages. Two otherwise useful photos could not meet the 88 KB detail-image limit without visible loss and were dropped rather than over-compressed.

Small crops removed only overlays: creator handles or footers on six graphics, a watermark strip on two owner photos, camera information strips on VOOK photos, and the drafter details beside one geometry table. No bike detail was altered.

New public posts may exist beyond these saved files; a later review should start from fresh saved posts.

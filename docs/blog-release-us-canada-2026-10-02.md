# US and Canada buying-guide release review

Reviewed October 2, 2026, Asia/Singapore (October 1 UTC), for only `buy-chinese-bikes-us-canada`. Original research began September 22; original owner-report dates and model identities remain historical. No price, stock, universal duty rate, delivery promise or hands-on test is added.

## Current routes and policies

- [Winspace dealer directory](https://www.winspace.cc/pages/authorized-dealers) and [SEKA global dealers](https://www.sekabikes.com/en/seka-global-dealer/) still list US and Canadian dealers. Directory presence is not stock confirmation.
- [Elves terms](https://elvesbike.com/terms.php) still directs buyers to the brand or country distributors and treats quotes as conditional. No country-specific availability is inferred.
- [YOELEO shipping](https://www.yoeleobike.com/pages/shipping-and-delivery) still describes worldwide shipping and estimated delivery. Its prepaid-tax list names the United States but not Canada. All three editions now make that distinction; no actual Canadian fee is inferred.
- [ICAN US wheel collection](https://icancycling.com/collections/carbon-disc-wheels-usa) still labels selected wheel products as US warehouse items. This remains explicitly separate from frame-stock evidence.
- [LightCarbon ordering and warranty FAQ](https://www.lightcarbon.com/warranty_d10) still offers manufacturer inquiries. Warranty eligibility and order costs require the exact purchase terms; the article makes no blanket support promise.
- [Tavelo official dealer page](https://tavelo.cc/pages/dealer) remains reachable; wording now asks buyers to confirm the regional seller instead of implying verified local stock.
- [PARDUS dealers](https://pardusbicycle.com/dealers/) remains a searchable global directory. No US or Canadian dealer is invented.
- [VeloBuild order instructions](https://www.velobuild.com/place-order-a0025.html) describe written confirmation and restrictive production/return terms. All editions now tell buyers to review these before payment.
- [Dengfu manufacturer page](https://m.dengfubikes.com/about/) still describes order confirmation, stock allocation or production, and dispatch. No stock or timing is assumed.
- [CBP Article 1919](https://www.help.cbp.gov/s/article/Article-1919?language=en_US), cross-checked against its indexed official text and [CBP duty-waiver guidance](https://www.help.cbp.gov/s/article/Article-1050), states the August 29, 2025 de minimis suspension. The direct article returned a script/CSS shell in text extraction; that failure was not treated as a policy change. The guide avoids an order-specific duty calculation.
- CBSA [duty/tax guidance](https://www.cbsa-asfc.gc.ca/import/courier/menu-eng.html) and [payment guidance](https://www.cbsa-asfc.gc.ca/import/postal-postale/dtytx-drttx-eng.html) remain indexed at the linked official URLs; direct initial fetches timed out. The current [2026 chapter 87](https://www.cbsa-asfc.gc.ca/trade-commerce/tariff-tarif/2026/html/02/ch87-eng.html) was retrieved and distinguishes bicycles from parts. Keep tax, duty and carrier charges separate and obtain a current destination-specific quote.

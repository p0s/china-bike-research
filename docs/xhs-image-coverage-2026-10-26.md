# XHS image coverage pass — 26 October 2026

This pass started from every catalog model whose card had no displayable photo:
one published platform and 77 research candidates. Nine GIRDER candidates already
show reviewed gallery photos on their model pages, and 18 rows need an exact model
or generation before any photo could be matched. The remaining exact models are
reviewed in batches of up to ten, one public post at a time.

Captures were images-only. Archives were validated and compactly assessed before
their text or images were read. Originals stay local; each public record keeps the
canonical post link, archive and original-image hashes, attribution, derivative
hashes and sizes, and a privacy review. Only resizing, metadata removal and
compression were applied. No specification, price or publication status changed.

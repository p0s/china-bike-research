Public titles, channel IDs, publication dates and descriptions establish the model/context match and disclosures. No full-playback, transcript or technical-claim audit is asserted. Raw metadata stays in ignored local research storage; no media, thumbnails, captions, comments, contact details or affiliate URLs enter Git.

## Identity and disclosure limits

The NorCal T1550 video did not establish Gen 2 in its description and was excluded. A 15-second Winspace France promotion added little to the longer Gen 2 build discussion and was omitted. The Jourdain LightCarbon first look explicitly linked LCG071-D and was rejected for LCG071-PRO. Tom Bowers and the LightCarbon product presentation explicitly identify LCG071-PRO and link its exact product page; neither is transferred to LCG071S-PRO. Falath EVO is kept separate from EVO+/EXP, CIMA road from CIMA GR, and original T1600 from T1600 Ultra.

Supplier discounts or affiliate links are stated without assuming frame supply. The T1600 review explicitly says Winspace supplied the frame, wheels and handlebars. The CIMA long-term description says Evolve gave the frame free but did not pay for the video; the channel’s retailer relationship remains disclosed. Daryl Pace’s brief CFR707 description establishes no ownership or commercial terms, so a new unknown relationship label preserves that uncertainty.

Creator-published chapters are linked for five videos: CFR707 fit/weight/ride, T1600 build/ride/conclusion, CIMA use/issues/current build, the CIMA component walkthrough, and GR:ONE wheels/issues/cockpit. They are navigation aids, not accepted measurements, fit advice or conclusions. Chapter labels, summaries, disclosures and the unknown-relationship label have reviewed Chinese and German copy. Original titles and canonical identities remain unchanged.

# Simplified Chinese localization

English URLs are retained; Chinese pages use `/zh/` and `lang="zh-Hans"`.
`assets/i18n.js` contains reviewed exact-source text pairs shared by the build and UI.
Article translations live alongside their English version in `content/posts/*.json`.
Product names and technical identifiers are retained. Source titles, exact seller wording,
and untranslated long-form research notes remain in the original language and are disclosed
on Chinese model pages; they are not guessed or silently rewritten.

The bilingual test requires a Chinese translation for every published model verdict. When
changing a verdict, update its exact-text translation in the same change. Source and
observation dates must not be refreshed merely because text was translated.

Long-form source/evidence translation is not complete. Extend exact-text mappings after
checking qualifiers, generation, price basis and units against the original record. Never
mass-replace English fragments inside technical model numbers or quoted evidence.

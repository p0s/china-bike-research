# Blog illustrations

Five original illustrations generated with OpenAI's built-in image-generation tool on 2026-09-22: one blog banner and four article covers. They depict generic cycling subjects and are not photographs of the exact models discussed or specification evidence.

The final prompt for each image, original content hash, localized alt text, credit, dimensions, byte counts and derivative hashes are recorded in [the image manifest](../../content/blog-images.json). WebP derivatives at 640 and 1600 pixels serve article cards and covers; 1200-pixel JPEGs serve sharing previews. All published files have embedded metadata removed. The privacy checker pins the approved file hashes.

Visual direction: natural light, muted greens and warm workshop materials. Keep text in HTML, outside the images. Article captions link to the image credits, and unavailable images leave the article text and links visible.

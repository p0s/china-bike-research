## Important limitations

- A remote image can disappear, move, reject hotlinking, or change without notice. The site falls back to original local artwork when loading fails.
- `official-page-embed` and `retailer-page-embed` mean the file is referenced from the cited host. They do not claim permission to download, redistribute, or relicense it.
- A frame-platform photograph may not show the listed drivetrain, wheels, cockpit, color, or finishing kit. The website labels this at the point of use.
- Every published platform currently has a credited product reference. The TRINX GTR C6 reference is explicitly labelled as a different international-market build and does not depict the China-market carbon specification.
- Before self-hosting a third-party photo, record explicit permission or a compatible license in its image record.

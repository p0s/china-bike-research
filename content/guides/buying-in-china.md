## Start with a decision specification

Write the requirements before opening Taobao or JD. At minimum: total budget, complete bike or frameset, drop or flat bar, minimum **measured** tire capability, preferred drivetrain, bottom-bracket preference, UDH requirement, mounting points, and whether a mainland warranty matters.

A good example is more useful than “cheap carbon gravel bike”:

> Complete drop-bar carbon bike, under ¥6,500, hydraulic brakes, 40 mm stock tire with enough dry clearance, no press-fit preference, mainland return route, and no unapproved component substitutions.

## Search using exact Chinese terms

Search the English model, Chinese brand name, model number, and category synonyms. Useful terms include `碳纤维砾石公路车`, `瓜车`, `Gravel`, `最大胎宽`, `实测胎宽`, `车架组`, `开箱`, `拆解`, `五通`, `尾钩`, and `质保`.

Use official pages to identify the platform, marketplaces to identify the exact configuration, and Chinese video/social sources to find measurements, recurring complaints, and actual delivered components. None of those sources should be treated as sufficient alone.

## Freeze the exact SKU in writing

Marketplace titles often group several builds, years, colors, and frames under one product page. Before paying, get the seller to confirm:

- model year and frame generation;
- size and cockpit dimensions;
- every drivetrain and brake component;
- wheel material and rim internal width;
- tire model and nominal size;
- bottom bracket, axles, and hanger;
- frame serial number and warranty entity;
- whether “same-level substitutions” are allowed.

Ask the seller to place the confirmation in the order notes or platform chat. A screenshot is useful for your own transaction, but do not publish account or order information in this repository.

## Treat price as a dated observation

A checkout total can depend on a store coupon, platform subsidy, membership, trade-in, payment method, color, size, or festival. Record the date and conditions. Do not tell another buyer that a bike “costs ¥5,191” without saying when and under what conditions it was observed.

## Protect the return window

Do not immediately discard packaging, cut a steerer, permanently route accessories, or ride rough terrain. First verify the serial, BOM, size, visual condition, brake/axle alignment, headset and bottom-bracket behavior, and the claimed tire space. See the [new-bike inspection guide](../new-bike-inspection/).

## Decide using total ownership, not the headline component

Electronic shifting can be extraordinary value, but include battery availability, crash replacement, firmware/app dependence, and local service. A better-documented frame can be worth more, but “T800” alone is not proof. Domestic warranty can be useful, but only if the seller and warranty entity are real and reachable.

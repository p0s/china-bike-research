---
title: Inspecting a new carbon bike
description: A return-window checklist for identity, damage, alignment, assembly, and first rides.
reviewed: 2026-08-06
---

## Before assembly

Photograph the sealed package, shipping damage, labels, serial number, and all included parts. Compare the delivered frame color, size, model year, and components with the written order. Keep every box, foam piece, axle spacer, and accessory.

Inspect the frame and fork under strong, changing-angle light. Look for impact marks, cracks, paint displacement around joints, poorly seated inserts, abnormal gaps, and damaged threads. Cosmetic imperfections are not automatically structural, but anything suspicious should be documented before riding.

## Interfaces and alignment

Check that both thru-axles thread smoothly without forcing, wheels sit centrally, rotors can be aligned without extreme caliper position, the headset turns smoothly without play, and the crank rotates without obvious binding. Brake mounts, hanger alignment, head-tube faces, and bottom-bracket tolerances may need a competent shop.

Do not “solve” a badly aligned new frame by permanently modifying parts while a return remains possible.

## Assembly safety

Use a calibrated torque wrench, correct carbon assembly paste where specified, and the manufacturer’s torque values. Confirm the expander plug supports the stem-clamp area on a carbon steerer. Do not exceed minimum or maximum insertion marks. Route hoses and wires without sharp bends or rub points.

## Tires and clearance

Inflate the intended tire on the intended rim, let it settle, measure actual width, and inspect the smallest gap while rotating both wheels. Check wheel dish and true. Do not assume that a stock nominal size proves the frame has enough margin for mud or debris.

## Controlled first rides

Start with a quiet, smooth ride. Recheck headset play, axle torque, rotor rub, shifting, seatpost movement, and unusual sounds. Increase load and terrain gradually. Stop for a new crack, sudden creak associated with load, visible movement, brake change, or steering abnormality.

## Preserve evidence

Communicate through the marketplace where possible, use clear photos/video, and state the requested remedy. Public owner reports are valuable after personal, account, shipping, and order identifiers have been removed.
